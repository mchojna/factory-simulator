# Engine

The engine: the rules of the factory on frozen dataclasses.

## Contents

- [__init__.py](__init__.py): The engine: the rules of the factory on frozen dataclasses.
- [changes.py](changes.py): The changes of a step: its ledger, its events and the numbers of an identifier.
- [checks/](checks/README.md): the checks subdirectory.
- [decisions/](decisions/README.md): the decisions subdirectory.
- [description.py](description.py): The description of a run: the factory of the run with its drawn values.
- [draws/](draws/README.md): the draws subdirectory.
- [economics.py](economics.py): The economic profile of a generated manifest: lowest costs, margins and coverage.
- [generation.py](generation.py): The manifest generated from counts of objects and a seed: its table and its rules.
- [measures/](measures/README.md): the measures subdirectory.
- [models/](models/README.md): the models subdirectory.
- [money.py](money.py): The money of a run: the interest on cash and the salvage value at the end.
- [numbers.py](numbers.py): The numbers of the engine: decimal context, its decorator, rounding, input error.
- [rules/](rules/README.md): the rules subdirectory.
- [steps/](steps/README.md): the steps subdirectory.
- [traces.py](traces.py): The trace snapshots: copies of values and the transitions of whole steps.

## Entry point

Start with [__init__.py](__init__.py).
