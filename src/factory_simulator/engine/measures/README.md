# Engine measures

The measures: the values a closed period records.

## Contents

- [__init__.py](__init__.py): The measures: the values a closed period records.
- [closed.py](closed.py): A closed period as the measures read it: state, orders, production, shared functions.
- [flows.py](flows.py): The flows of a period: customers, costs, routes, lines, inputs, suppliers, decisions.
- [groups.py](groups.py): The measures of groups: sums and means over their members.
- [kinds.py](kinds.py): The kinds of the measures: flows, levels and ratios.
- [levels.py](levels.py): The levels of a period: cash, stock, space and condition.
- [period.py](period.py): The measures of a period: the first totals and the measures recorded at each close.

## Entry point

Start with [__init__.py](__init__.py).
