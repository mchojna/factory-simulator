# API services readers

The readers: bodies into the dataclasses of the engine, one module per body.

## Contents

- [__init__.py](__init__.py): The readers: bodies into the dataclasses of the engine, one module per body.
- [decisions.py](decisions.py): The reader of a submission: the decisions of the engine, with defaults of the run.
- [manifest.py](manifest.py): The reader of a manifest: the factory of the engine and the content as written.
- [scenario.py](scenario.py): The reader of a scenario: the scenario of the engine, its shapes and disruptions.
